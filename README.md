# Health Log: putting it on your iPhone

A private, offline journal for food, bowel movements and symptoms, with an analysis page that looks for patterns and a printable report for your doctor.

Each person installs it on their own phone. **Entries are stored only on that phone**: there are no accounts and no server, and nobody else (including other family members) can see them.

> These files contain **no personal data**. Your entries exist only inside the app on your phone.

## 1. Install it

1. Open the app's web address in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. **Close Safari and always open the app from the Home Screen icon.** Safari tabs and the installed app keep separate storage.

## 2. Using it

The four tabs along the bottom:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| 🍔 **Food** | Pick the date, time and meal (Breakfast, AM Snack, Lunch, PM Snack, Dinner, Evening Snack). Search for foods and drinks or tap one from ★ Favorites / Frequent. Remove an item with ✕, then tap **Save**. The meal you picked stays selected, so you can add several items in a row. Tap ☆ on any item to make it a favorite. |
| 💩 **Stool** | Date and time (defaults to now), the stool type (Bristol Stool Scale 1–7), the amount (light / medium / heavy), plus optional blood, mucus, urgency, pain or incomplete emptying. |
| ➕ **Health** | Tap where you feel it on the front or back figure to zoom in. Then **📍 Pin** a spot or **⭕ Circle** an area, and choose the symptoms and how strong they are (1–10). **🧍 Whole body** is for fatigue, chills, fever, brain fog and similar. |
| 📊 **Analysis** | Possible food triggers, recurring cycles, time of day, day of week, time of month, a body heat-map, top symptoms and bowel-movement trends. Tap **Create a doctor report** to print or save a PDF. |

New foods you type are saved with tags (dairy, gluten, nightshades, etc.). The tags are what let the analysis spot ingredients that may be triggers.

## 3. How the analysis works

- It compares days **after** you had a food or ingredient (same day, next day, 2 days later) with days you didn't.
- Your own data is shuffled many times to see how strong a pattern looks **by pure chance**. Only patterns that beat chance are shown, labelled **Possible**, **Moderate** or **Strong**.
- Foods eaten together are separated. Once the strongest trigger is found, its effect is removed before looking for the next one.
- **Recurring cycles** (for example "every ~28 days") are found from your symptom scores alone, with no period tracking needed, so they work for everyone. A monthly rhythm needs about 3 months of logging.

It shows patterns, not causes. Talk to your doctor before cutting out foods.

## 4. Back up regularly

**⚙︎ Settings → Back up now** → save the file to iCloud Drive or email it to yourself. If the app is removed, its data goes with it, and a backup is the only way to get it back (**Settings → Restore from a backup**). The app reminds you after a week.

## Updating the app

Push new files to the repository. Phones download the update the next time the app is opened online and show it the time after that. Entries are not affected.
